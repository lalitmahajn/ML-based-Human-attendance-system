Quality Check

Check the following for rejected samples. This page does not add a new backend check.

  • Blur: wipe lens, increase lighting and retake.
  • Face size: face should be centered and large enough.
  • Pose: turn slowly following instructions, do not cover face.
  • Alignment score: if low, look straight ahead and capture again.
  • Duplicate risk: if similarity with another employee is reported, check the profile.
